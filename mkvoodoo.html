Control_02

Built for local control

MKVoodoo is designed for people who want their media workflow to stay on their machine. No upload step, no account ritual, no cloud dependency. Your folders stay yours.

Offline-first

Conversions run locally through FFmpeg, with no remote processing path in the workflow.

Track-aware

Preserve all audio and subtitle tracks, or pick exactly what each output file should carry.

Hardware-ready

Detect NVIDIA NVENC and Intel Quick Sync where available, with CPU encoding as fallback.

Queue-safe

Skip existing files, retry failures, reset failed jobs, and clean completed history.
