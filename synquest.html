A world that remembers
what happened.

Malevolent: Scorned is a small experimental game project. Its focus is not spectacle, but the tension created when a world holds on to choices, scars, and unfinished stories.

01

Persistent world

Locations, encounters, and consequences are designed to remember what a player leaves behind.

02

Narrative voice

An AI-guided storyteller is explored as a way to make the world feel responsive without losing its tone.

03

Experimental by nature

The project remains a creative research space, free to evolve as the underlying ideas become clearer.

Current status: exploratory development. This page documents the project direction, not a public release commitment.
