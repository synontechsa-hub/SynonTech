Philosophy

Built for Production

🛡️

Local Independence

Your workshop data belongs in your workshop. KerfSuite apps operate locally, ensuring your pricing, inventory, and job history stay on your machine—no cloud dependency.

🔗

Seamless Integration

The suite is built to talk to itself. Optimize a cut in KerfCut, and KerfStock automatically marks that material as consumed. One source of truth for the entire shop floor.

⚡

Rapid Response

Engineered for speed. From background-threaded optimization to lightweight database lookups, the suite stays snappy even when managing thousands of stock items.
