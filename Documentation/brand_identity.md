# SYNONTECH Brand Identity Guide

Version 1.0

---

# Overview

Synontech is the software and technology division operating under the Byte This Games umbrella.

Where Byte This Games focuses on creativity, games, virtual worlds, and digital experiences, Synontech exists to build practical, professional software products that solve real-world problems.

Synontech is the engineering arm of the ecosystem.

---

# Mission

Build useful software that empowers people to work smarter, create more, and focus on what matters.

---

# Vision

To become a respected independent software company known for thoughtful design, reliable products, and user-first development.

---

# Core Values

## Practical Innovation

Technology should solve problems.

We prioritize usefulness over hype and functionality over buzzwords.

## Craftsmanship

Software should feel deliberate.

Every feature should have a purpose.

Every product should feel polished.

## Independence

We build products because we believe in them.

Not because investors demand them.

Not because trends tell us to.

## Trust

User trust is earned through reliability, transparency, and respect.

## Continuous Growth

Small improvements compound into exceptional products.

---

# Brand Personality

Synontech should feel:

* Professional
* Modern
* Intelligent
* Reliable
* Approachable
* Focused

Synontech should NOT feel:

* Corporate
* Overly formal
* Cyberpunk
* Aggressive
* Trend-chasing
* Gimmicky

# Brand Positioning
