The Core Suite

v1.0.4 // ACTIVE

KerfCut

Algorithmic cut-list optimizer designed to maximize material yield. KerfCut takes your piece lists and packs them into raw sheets using MaxRects and Guillotine logic, generating instant quotes and PDF cut plans.

  • Smart Sheet Packing
  • Instant Quoting
  • Grain Direction Locking
  • PDF Cut Plan Export
Download KerfCut
v0.8.2 // BETA

KerfStock

Real-time workshop inventory tracking. KerfStock monitors sheet goods, hardware, and offcut inventory, alerting you when stock is low and integrating directly with KerfCut for material selection.

  • Inventory Monitoring
  • Offcut Management
  • Low Stock Alerts
  • KerfCut Integration

Philosophy

Built for Production

🛡️

Local Independence

Your workshop data belongs in your workshop. KerfSuite apps operate locally, ensuring your pricing, inventory, and job history stay on your machine—no cloud dependency.

🔗

Seamless Integration

The suite is built to talk to itself. Optimize a cut in KerfCut, and KerfStock automatically marks that material as consumed. One source of truth for the entire shop floor.

⚡

Rapid Response

Engineered for speed. From background-threaded optimization to lightweight database lookups, the suite stays snappy even when managing thousands of stock items.
