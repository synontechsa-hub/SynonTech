What it does

A complete media cleanup workflow, in one place.

MKVoodoo handles the repetitive work between finding a video and putting a clean, correctly named file into your library.

01

Convert in batches

Queue whole folders, choose a preset, and let FFmpeg work through the list while you keep an eye on progress.

02

Download from YouTube

Bring supported videos into the same local workflow, with the downloader updated for the v1.1.1 release.

03

Name files intelligently

Review detected season and episode details before output, so a messy source becomes a tidy media library.

04

Control every track

Preserve all audio and subtitles, or select exactly which streams should travel into the final file.

05

Use your hardware

Take advantage of NVIDIA NVENC or Intel Quick Sync where available, with dependable CPU fallback.

06

Recover the queue

Retry failures, skip existing outputs, clear completed jobs, and get a long batch moving again without starting over.

A calmer workflow

Four steps from source to sorted.

Everything important stays visible before you start the queue, so you spend less time fixing surprises afterwards.

  1. 01

    Add

    Choose videos, drop in a folder, or paste a supported download link.

  2. 02

    Review

    Confirm names, tracks, source details, and the intended destination.

  3. 03

    Tune

    Select resolution, quality, encoder, and stream-handling preferences.

  4. 04

    Run

    Start the queue, watch progress, and retry individual jobs if needed.

Local by design

Your media stays on your machine.

Conversion is handled locally through FFmpeg. There is no upload step, no account requirement, and no remote conversion service between you and your files.
